\textbf{Proposition:} For all integers $a$ and $b$, $4a+5b+3$ is even.

\textbf{Proof:} Let $a$ and $b$ be integers. Then, let $s=4a+5b+3$. Hence,

\begin{align*}
s&=4a+5b+3\\
&=4a+4b+b+2+1\\
&=2(2a+2b+1)+b+1
\end{align*}

Notice that by choosing $b$ to be even, we can make $s$ be odd.
In this case, let $b=2k$ for some integer $k$.
Then, we have

\begin{align*}
s&=2(2a+2(2k)+1)+2k+1\\
&=2(2a+5k+1)+1
\end{align*}

Thus, by definition, $s$ is odd. Therefore, by finding a family of counterexamples, we
have shown that the original statement is false. \qed
