\begin{proposition}
Prove the language $\{a^{2^n}|n \ge 0\}$ is nonregular.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
Let $A=\{a^{2^n}|n\ge0\}$. Assume, for the sake of contradiction,
$A$ is regular. Then, there exists a pumping length $p$.

Let
$w=a^{2^p}$. Then, $w \in A$, and $|w|=2^p \ge p$. By the Pumping
Lemma, there exists strings $x$, $y$, and $z$ such that $w=xyz$. Furthermore, $xy^iz \in A$ for every integer $i \ge 0$.

Let $|y|=c$. Recall that, by the Pumping Lemma, $|y|>0$. So,
\begin{equation}
\label{ine}
0 < c \le 2^p
\end{equation}

Note that
\begin{align*}
|xyyz|&=|xyz|+|y|\\
&=2^p+c
\end{align*}

By inequality (\ref{ine}), we have
\begin{equation}
\label{ine2}
2^p < 2^p+c \le 2^{p+1}
\end{equation}

Since $xyyz \in A$, $|xyyz|$ is a power of 2. By inequality (\ref{ine2}),
we have $c=2^p$.

Now consider
\begin{align*}
|xyyyz|&=|xyz|+|y|+|y|\\
&=2^p+2c\\
&=2^p+2 \cdot 2^p\\
&=3 \cdot 2^p
\end{align*}

Since $|xyyyz|$ is not a power of 2, it follows that $xyyyz \notin A$. This contradicts
the Pumping Lemma. Hence, we can conclude $A$ is nonregular.
\end{proof}
