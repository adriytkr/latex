\begin{problem}
Prove $n!+2$ is divisible by $2$ for every integer $n \ge 2$.
\end{problem}

\begin{proof}
Let $n$ be an integer with $n \ge 2$. Then

\begin{align*}
n!+2&=n(n-1)\cdots2\cdot1+2\\
&=2(n(n+1)\cdots3+1)
\end{align*}

Since $n!+2$ has a factor of 2, $n!+2$ is divisible by $2$.
\end{proof}
